What this shows: scanning and attack traffic seen by honeypots and firewall sensors in the SANS Internet Storm Center's DShield network, for the last complete day. These are sensor observations, mostly automated scanning and password guessing (many of the busiest sources are internet-wide research or commercial scanners), not confirmed incidents, and they are never added to the claims or reported attacks elsewhere on this dashboard. Source country is where the source IP's network is registered, not where the attacker is. DShield does not publish where its sensors are, so arcs end at the dashboard's Europe focus point, not at a real target. The region switch filters by source network location. Inspired by the T-Pot Attack Map.
